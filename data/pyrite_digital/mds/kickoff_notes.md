## Key Decisions Made

### 1. Office Expansion and Infrastructure
**Decision**: Lease additional 2,000 sq ft office space
- **Rationale**: Current space at 80% capacity, need room for team growth
- **Investment**: ₹8 lakhs setup cost, ₹1.2 lakhs monthly rent
- **Timeline**: New space operational by November 1
- **Owner**: Sathish

### 2. Technology Stack Upgrade
**Decision**: Invest ₹15 lakhs in new technology and tools
- **Creative Suite**: Upgrade to latest Adobe Creative Cloud enterprise
- **Analytics Platform**: Custom dashboard development for client reporting
- **Project Management**: Implement Monday.com for enhanced workflow
- **Owner**: Arjun Mehta (Creative), Priya Nair (Analytics)

### 3. Client Tier Restructuring
**Decision**: Implement new client tier system with specialized service levels
- **Tier 1** (₹5L+ monthly): Dedicated account manager, weekly strategy calls
- **Tier 2** (₹2-5L monthly): Bi-weekly check-ins, monthly strategy reviews
- **Tier 3** (₹50K-2L monthly): Monthly reporting, quarterly strategy sessions
- **Owner**: Sneha Patel

### 4. Performance Incentive Program
**Decision**: Launch team performance bonus program
- **Structure**: Quarterly bonuses based on client performance metrics
- **Criteria**: ROAS targets, client satisfaction scores, retention rates
- **Budget**: ₹5 lakhs quarterly bonus pool
- **Owner**: Sathish (with HR support)

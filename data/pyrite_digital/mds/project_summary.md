# Pyrite Digital - Q3 2024 Growth Campaign Portfolio

## Agency Overview

Pyrite Digital is a performance-focused creative agency specializing in D2C brand growth through integrated digital marketing strategies. Founded by Sathish in 2022, we've grown from a 2-person startup to a 15-member team serving 25+ D2C brands across fashion, beauty, food, and lifestyle categories.

## Core Service Offerings

### Performance Marketing
- **Facebook & Instagram Ads**: Advanced targeting and creative optimization
- **Google Ads**: Search, Shopping, and Display campaign management
- **Influencer Marketing**: Micro and macro influencer partnership programs
- **Email Marketing**: Automated sequences and retention campaigns

### Creative Services
- **Brand Strategy**: Positioning, messaging, and visual identity development
- **Content Production**: Photography, videography, and graphic design
- **Social Media Management**: Organic content strategy and community building
- **Website Development**: Conversion-optimized e-commerce experiences

### Analytics & Optimization
- **Performance Tracking**: Custom dashboards and reporting systems
- **Conversion Rate Optimization**: A/B testing and user experience improvements
- **Customer Journey Mapping**: Multi-touchpoint attribution and optimization
- **Market Research**: Competitive analysis and consumer insights

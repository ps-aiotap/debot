## Project Vision Statement

*"To create India's most sustainable and community-focused township that sets new standards for integrated living while respecting local culture and environment."*

## Key Decisions Made

### 1. Phased Development Approach
**Decision**: Implement 4-phase development over 7 years
- **Rationale**: Manage cash flow and reduce market risk
- **Owner**: Ajay Sir
- **Timeline**: Phase 1 launch by December 2024

### 2. Sustainability Focus
**Decision**: Target IGBC Gold certification for entire township
- **Rationale**: Market differentiation and long-term value
- **Owner**: Sunil Kumar (Construction)
- **Timeline**: Certification process begins immediately

### 3. Community Integration Strategy
**Decision**: Establish local employment program (30% local hiring)
- **Rationale**: Build community support and reduce opposition
- **Owner**: Priya Sharma
- **Timeline**: Program launch with Phase 1 construction

### 4. Premium Positioning
**Decision**: Position as premium township with competitive pricing
- **Rationale**: Balance quality aspirations with market accessibility
- **Owner**: Meera Joshi
- **Timeline**: Pricing strategy finalized by April 15

## Risk Assessment

### High Priority Risks
1. **Regulatory Delays**: Government approval timeline uncertainty
2. **Land Acquisition**: Potential holdouts or price escalation
3. **Market Conditions**: Real estate market volatility
4. **Infrastructure Costs**: Utility connection cost overruns

### Medium Priority Risks
1. **Environmental Compliance**: Ongoing monitoring requirements
2. **Construction Quality**: Managing multiple contractors
3. **Community Relations**: Maintaining local support
4. **Competition**: New projects in similar locations

### Low Priority Risks
1. **Weather Delays**: Monsoon impact on construction
2. **Material Costs**: Construction material price fluctuation
3. **Talent Retention**: Key team member availability
4. **Technology Integration**: Smart township features implementation

# Viswak Township Development - Phase 1 Project Summary

## Project Overview

Viswak Realty is developing a 500-acre integrated township in Nashik, Maharashtra, targeting middle-income families with modern amenities and sustainable infrastructure. This flagship project represents our expansion from individual residential projects to comprehensive community development.

## Project Details

### Location & Scale
- **Site**: Nashik-Pune Highway, 25km from Nashik city center
- **Total Area**: 500 acres (202 hectares)
- **Development Phases**: 4 phases over 7 years
- **Total Units**: 2,500 residential units + commercial spaces
- **Investment**: ₹1,200 crores total project cost

### Target Demographics
- **Primary**: Middle-income families (₹8-15 lakhs annual income)
- **Secondary**: Young professionals working in Nashik IT corridor
- **Tertiary**: Retirees seeking peaceful community living

## Land Acquisition Strategy

### Acquisition Timeline
- **Phase 1**: 125 acres acquired (completed)
- **Phase 2**: 150 acres under negotiation
- **Phase 3**: 125 acres identified, preliminary discussions
- **Phase 4**: 100 acres reserved for future expansion

### Acquisition Challenges
1. **Fragmented Ownership**: 47 different landowners across target area
2. **Agricultural Land Conversion**: NA permission required for 380 acres
3. **Price Negotiations**: Land rates increased 40% during acquisition period
4. **Documentation Issues**: Title verification for 12 parcels pending

### Stakeholder Management
- **Farmers**: Direct negotiations with transparent pricing
- **Local Leaders**: Community engagement through village panchayats
- **Government Officials**: Regular liaison with collector's office
- **Environmental Groups**: Proactive engagement on sustainability measures

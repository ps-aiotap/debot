# iThrive Patient Onboarding Flow
## Comprehensive Health Transformation Journey

**Document Owner**: Avinash, CEO

## Overview

The iThrive onboarding process is designed to create a seamless transition from initial inquiry to active health transformation. Our approach ensures every patient receives personalized attention while maintaining operational efficiency and clinical excellence.

## Phase 1: Initial Engagement (Days 1-3)

### Step 1: Health Assessment Questionnaire
**Duration**: 45-60 minutes  
**Platform**: iThrive digital assessment portal  

**Components**:
- **Medical History**: Comprehensive review of past and current health conditions
- **Symptom Analysis**: Detailed symptom mapping and severity assessment
- **Lifestyle Evaluation**: Diet, exercise, sleep, and stress patterns
- **Goals Setting**: Short-term and long-term health objectives
- **Motivation Assessment**: Understanding patient's readiness for change

**Automated Outputs**:
- Risk stratification score (Low/Medium/High complexity)
- Preliminary protocol recommendations
- Estimated transformation timeline
- Investment requirement calculation

### Step 2: Biomarker Testing Coordination
**Timeline**: Scheduled within 48 hours of assessment completion  

**Standard Panel**:
- **Metabolic Markers**: HbA1c, fasting glucose, insulin levels
- **Lipid Profile**: Total cholesterol, HDL, LDL, triglycerides
- **Inflammatory Markers**: CRP, ESR, homocysteine
- **Nutritional Status**: Vitamin D, B12, folate, iron studies
- **Thyroid Function**: TSH, T3, T4, reverse T3

**Advanced Testing** (based on assessment):
- **Food Sensitivity Panel**: IgG testing for 90+ foods
- **Micronutrient Analysis**: Comprehensive vitamin and mineral status
- **Toxic Metal Screen**: Heavy metal exposure assessment
- **Gut Health Analysis**: Comprehensive stool analysis with microbiome mapping

### Step 3: Practitioner Assignment
**Matching Criteria**:
- **Specialization**: Condition-specific expertise (diabetes, autoimmune, etc.)
- **Experience Level**: Complex cases assigned to senior practitioners
- **Communication Style**: Personality and approach compatibility
- **Availability**: Optimal scheduling for patient preferences

## Phase 2: Clinical Consultation (Days 4-7)

### Initial Consultation Session
**Duration**: 90 minutes (in-person or virtual)  
**Participants**: Patient, assigned nutritionist, health coach  

**Agenda**:
1. **Health History Deep Dive** (20 minutes)
   - Detailed review of assessment responses
   - Clarification of symptoms and health concerns
   - Family history and genetic predispositions
   - Previous treatment experiences and outcomes

2. **Biomarker Review and Analysis** (25 minutes)
   - Comprehensive lab result interpretation
   - Identification of key health imbalances
   - Root cause analysis and connections
   - Priority areas for intervention

3. **Personalized Protocol Development** (30 minutes)
   - Customized nutrition plan creation
   - Lifestyle modification recommendations
   - Supplement protocol design
   - Timeline and milestone setting

4. **Education and Empowerment** (15 minutes)
   - Understanding of functional medicine approach
   - Patient role in transformation process
   - Resource sharing and educational materials
   - Questions and concerns addressing

### Protocol Documentation
**Deliverables**:
- **Personalized Nutrition Plan**: Detailed meal guidelines and recipes
- **Supplement Protocol**: Specific products, dosages, and timing
- **Lifestyle Recommendations**: Sleep, exercise, and stress management
- **Monitoring Schedule**: Follow-up appointments and testing timeline

## Phase 3: Implementation Support (Days 8-30)

### Week 1: Intensive Support
**Daily Check-ins**: Via iThrive app or WhatsApp
**Focus Areas**:
- Meal planning and preparation guidance
- Supplement compliance and timing
- Initial symptom tracking and adjustments
- Motivation and encouragement

### Week 2-3: Guided Independence
**Support Frequency**: Every 2-3 days
**Activities**:
- Progress review and protocol refinements
- Challenge identification and problem-solving
- Habit formation and routine establishment
- Community group introduction

### Week 4: Milestone Assessment
**Comprehensive Review**:
- **Symptom Improvement**: Quantitative and qualitative assessment
- **Biomarker Changes**: Follow-up testing for key markers
- **Compliance Evaluation**: Protocol adherence and challenges
- **Protocol Adjustments**: Refinements based on initial response

## Phase 4: Ongoing Transformation (Month 2+)

### Monthly Progress Reviews
**Format**: 60-minute consultation (virtual or in-person)
**Components**:
- **Health Metrics Review**: Weight, energy, sleep, symptoms
- **Biomarker Tracking**: Quarterly comprehensive testing
- **Protocol Evolution**: Adjustments based on progress and goals
- **Education Advancement**: Advanced topics and strategies

### Quarterly Comprehensive Assessments
**Includes**:
- **Complete Biomarker Panel**: Full health status evaluation
- **Body Composition Analysis**: DEXA scan or InBody assessment
- **Functional Capacity Testing**: Energy, strength, and endurance
- **Quality of Life Metrics**: Validated questionnaires and surveys
